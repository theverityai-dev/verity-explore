# Film A1: "The business comes first" (9:8, dark cinematic)

Composition `Film-A1-Adapt-9x8`, 1080x960, 60fps, 73s. Code: `src/film/adapt/` (`tokens.tsx`, `Desk.tsx`, `BrandFilm.tsx`).
Render: `npm run render:film-a1`. Output: `renders/films/a1-adapt-9x8.mp4`. Replaces the rejected light-theme version.

Built to the premium enterprise brief. The environment is near-black (#0B0C0E), with light Verity UI, Inter, and the
accent #6E8FFF only for connections, approvals and Verity moments. There is no voiceover: timings follow the Hinglish
VO's timestamps, so the recording drops straight on. Sound is room tone plus quiet paper, click and glass cues.

**Through-line.** The business's own workflow graph (Request, Review, Approve / More info loop, Quotation, Order,
Execution) is born from the desk and keeps one layout (L0) for the whole film. Verity never moves a node; the software
reshapes around it. The graph at 0:09 and at 1:00 is identical, which is the callback.
