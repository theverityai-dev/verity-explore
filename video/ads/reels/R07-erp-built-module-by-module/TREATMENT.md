# R07 treatment: "Built around your business"

VO: founder, `vo/picked/R07-founder-take1.wav` (40.8 s, Hinglish). Word timings: `vo/words.json` (faster-whisper medium).
Film length: about 43.5 s (VO plus a 2.8 s end-card hold). Canvas 1080 x 1920, 30 fps. Theme: light.

## Creative concept

**The Verity Blueprint.** A quiet white architectural studio. A business arrives as fragments on standing acrylic
planes. A fixed system tries to force them into one lattice; they refuse, because each business has its own shape. Verity's
product managers lay a drafting sheet over one business and trace how it really works. The tracing becomes an exploded
stack of layers (workflows, teams, approvals, reporting, requirements), and the stack is built into a Verity workspace,
module by module. The same drafting sheet then becomes the offer: the Business Blueprint. One object, the blueprint,
carries the film and is the thing being sold.

## World

- **Space:** a pale studio room seen at eye level. Wall `#F2F4F7`, floor `#E8EBEF`, soft horizon at y 1180. No props
  other than the planes, the sheet and the workspace.
- **Materials:** frosted acrylic planes (translucent white face, 1 px white rim, visible 8 px edge thickness, soft cast
  shadow). Matte drafting film for the blueprint (cool white, blue 24/120 px drafting grid at 5-9 %, registration marks,
  title block). Real Verity UI, light theme, for the workspace.
- **Lighting:** one large diffused key from upper right. A wide soft light shaft falls across the wall at 24 degrees.
  Shadows fall down and to the left, long and soft (ink 8-12 %, 18-30 px blur). No coloured light, no glow.
- **Lens and camera:** 85 mm equivalent (perspective 2400 px, so planes turn without distortion). One continuous dolly
  forward through the studio over the whole film, plus lateral parallax between depth layers (background at 0.4x).
- **Typography:** Inter, weight 300, sentence case, black ink. Hero 84-104 px at cap line y 380. Text lands once and
  stays still while the world moves. No per-word animation. Captions burned in at the bottom of the safe band.
- **Accent:** Verity blue only for the traced workflow path, the approval tick and the CTA.
- **Avoid:** cards flying in, generic SaaS diagrams, gradients that read as colour, glow, particles, elastic easing, UI
  popping from nowhere, stock people, laptops, handshakes.
