## Creative direction

**World.** A partner's desk edge, a row of matter folders with tabs along the top, and a wall calendar with a few dates ringed. Matte paper objects against the dark room.

**Hook, first 2 s.** Headline (draft, English, oversized): "Every client." / "Every deadline." / "One place?". Folder tabs lift one by one along the row; the wall calendar is behind them. Headline upper left.

**Signature animation.** Calendar dates get a thin arc ring that tightens toward each deadline. A folder tab lifts and an invoice slip stays paper-clipped to it, unpaid; the ring on its date keeps closing.

**Pain beat.** The phone thread, with a folder and calendar dimmed behind.

| Sender | Message (English, on screen) | Attachment chip |
|---|---|---|
| Associate | Did you follow up with the client? | Client note |
| Partner | When is that deadline? | Calendar invite |
| Accounts | Why is the invoice still pending? | INV-0931.pdf |

Followed by three short ones ("Sir?", "Urgent", "Please reply") and a 99+ badge. Chat list rows: Client A "Any update?", Associates "Draft ready", Accounts "Invoice pending", Partner "Deadline Friday?", Client B "Documents sent", Admin "Who files this?".

**Montage (the scattered tools, about 5 fast cuts, drawn as industry objects).** Client chat; Matter and deadline tracker sheet; Call log of follow-ups; Invoice list; Notebook of to-dos.

**Problem reveal.** The chaos freezes on "Business system?" with a drawn cross, held still. Then two headlines, A: "It isn't your team."  B: "Client work and ops" / "aren't connected."
Client, matter, deadline and invoice sit on four separate cards; thin lines chain them into one row.

**Verity system reveal (tailored; the rest of the tail is shared).** The mapped business turns into the real Verity interface for this industry, built module by module:
- Panel "Compliance": Obligations due, Not started, Open notices, Audits in fieldwork
- Attention row: "38 obligations due within 15 days not started" (illustrative figure, as in the product content)
- Workflow shown: "Registration to obligation calendar"
- Workspace modules (real capability names, `content/capabilities.js`): Work, Relationships, Records, Communication, Reports and analytics
- Analysis sheet bottom dimension: "Brief to invoice"
