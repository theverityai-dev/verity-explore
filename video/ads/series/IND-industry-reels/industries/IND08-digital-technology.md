## Creative direction

**World.** A sprint board with four columns of task cards, a timesheet strip below it with one bar per project and a thin estimate line across it, and a client-thread card to the right.

**Hook, first 2 s.** Headline (draft, English, oversized): "Which project" / "is on track?". The board with its columns and the timesheet strip beneath. Headline upper left.

**Signature animation.** Task cards move across the board columns on a steady beat. In the timesheet strip a project's hours bar grows and crosses the estimate line while its client update card has no reply, and the part past the line is the only accent.

**Pain beat.** The phone thread, with the board and timesheet strip dimmed behind it.

| Sender | Message (English, on screen) | Attachment chip |
|---|---|---|
| Client | Did you update the client? | Update draft |
| Lead | Is the task complete? | Ticket 214 |
| Finance | How did this project's hours get so high? | Timesheet |

Followed by three short ones ("Sir?", "Urgent", "Please reply") and a 99+ badge. Chat list rows: Client "Any update?", Dev lead "Task blocked", PM "Hours exceeded", Finance "Invoice ready?", Designer "Review needed", Support "Ticket escalated".

**Montage (the scattered tools, about 5 fast cuts, drawn as industry objects).** Client email thread; Task tracker; Timesheet; Invoice list; Stand-up notes.

**Problem reveal.** The chaos freezes on "Business system?" with a drawn cross, held still. Then two headlines, A: "It isn't the tools."  B: "Operations aren't" / "connected."
Tracker, client thread and timesheet are three tabs; they collapse onto one project row with its hours, estimate and acceptance.

**Verity system reveal (tailored; the rest of the tail is shared).** The mapped business turns into the real Verity interface for this industry, built module by module:
- Panel "Projects": Active projects, Over estimate, Milestones unaccepted, In warranty
- Attention row: "5 projects past estimate with no change request" (illustrative figure, as in the product content)
- Workflow shown: "Delivery and effort"
- Workspace modules (real capability names, `content/capabilities.js`): Work, Relationships, People, Communication, Reports and analytics
- Analysis sheet bottom dimension: "Brief to delivery"

## Keyframes to score before animating (8 or above, `verity-reel` gate)

1. Hook frame (0.9 s)  2. Pain frame with the thread  3. Montage frame (one of the five)  4. Freeze "Business system?"
5. Reframe frame with fragments or islands joining  6. System reveal, panel built  7. Offer frame (shared, check the strike)
8. End card (shared)

A frame that scores under 8 is redesigned before any motion is written.
