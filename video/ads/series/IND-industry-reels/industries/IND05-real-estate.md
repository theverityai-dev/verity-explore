## Creative direction

**World.** A layout plan board: a grid of plots or units with numbers, a few pins, a brochure edge and a key on the side. Drafting-film materials on the dark room, close to the Blueprint language.

**Hook, first 2 s.** Headline (draft, English, oversized): "Every lead." / "Every visit." / "Every deal.". The layout plan with all units pale. Pins drop. Headline upper left.

**Signature animation.** Pins drop onto the plan one by one with a smooth ease and unit tiles change status through a tween (available, visited, in negotiation). One pin has nobody following it and stays dim while its neighbours light.

**Pain beat.** The phone thread, with the plan board dimmed behind it.

| Sender | Message (English, on screen) | Attachment chip |
|---|---|---|
| Sales | Sir, did you call that client? | Lead note |
| Broker | When is the site visit? | Visit slot |
| Client | Is that property still available? | Unit B-204 |

Followed by three short ones ("Sir?", "Urgent", "Please reply") and a 99+ badge. Chat list rows: Lead "When is the visit?", Broker "Client asking price", Sales team "Unit still available?", Accounts "Booking amount?", Site engineer "Handover date?", Lead "Call me back".

**Montage (the scattered tools, about 5 fast cuts, drawn as industry objects).** Lead chat; Listings spreadsheet; Call log; Site-visit diary; Broker voice notes.

**Problem reveal.** The chaos freezes on "Business system?" with a drawn cross, held still. Then two headlines, A: "It isn't your leads."  B: "No proper system" / "manages them."
Lead, visit, negotiation and closing are four stations on the plan; a line walks a lead through them and the dim pin finally lights.

**Verity system reveal (tailored; the rest of the tail is shared).** The mapped business turns into the real Verity interface for this industry, built module by module:
- Panel "Pipeline": Active leads, Site visits, In negotiation, No contact 14d
- Attention row: "46 leads with no contact in 14 days" (illustrative figure, as in the product content)
- Workflow shown: "Site visit"
- Workspace modules (real capability names, `content/capabilities.js`): Relationships, Work, Locations, Suppliers, Reports and analytics
- Analysis sheet bottom dimension: "Lead to booking"

## Keyframes to score before animating (8 or above, `verity-reel` gate)

1. Hook frame (0.9 s)  2. Pain frame with the thread  3. Montage frame (one of the five)  4. Freeze "Business system?"
5. Reframe frame with fragments or islands joining  6. System reveal, panel built  7. Offer frame (shared, check the strike)
8. End card (shared)

A frame that scores under 8 is redesigned before any motion is written.
