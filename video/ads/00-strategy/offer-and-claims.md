# Offer and claims

Single source of truth for what any ad may say. If it is not here, it is not approved.

## The offer

**Verity Business Blueprint™**: a structured analysis of how a business actually works, ending in a proposed system
blueprint built around that business.

| Item | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Verity Business Blueprint™ | Chosen over Business Audit, Workflow Blueprint, Discovery |
| Stated value | ₹5,000 | **OPEN** (see D1) |
| Price now | FREE, limited time | **OPEN** (see D3) |
| Primary CTA | Get Your Free Business Blueprint → | Approved wording |
| Short CTA | Claim Your Free Blueprint → / Get Yours Free → | Approved wording |

### What the Blueprint covers (the 8 areas, usable as on-screen list)

Operations · Teams and responsibilities · Workflows · Approvals · Reporting · Existing software · Pain points ·
Automation opportunities. Output: a **proposed system blueprint** around the business.

### The process (usable as the mechanism visual)

Business → Workflows → Teams → Approvals → Requirements → Business Blueprint → Proposed system → Your approval →
Implementation.

## Approved claims

- Verity is a custom business management platform for growing businesses.
- Most business software ships with a fixed way of working. Verity starts with yours.
- We understand your business first, then configure the system around it.
- Requirement, then proposed solution, then implementation after your approval.
- No forced workflows. No unnecessary modules.
- No implementation commitment is required at the Blueprint stage. **(confirm with sales, D4)**
- Tell us how your business works; we show what your system could look like.
