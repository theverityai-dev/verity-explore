# Audience and funnel

## Who we talk to

Business owners and founders of growing Indian businesses (MSME to mid-size) who run operations through Excel,
WhatsApp, calls and disconnected tools. They are not buying software yet. They are deciding whether someone can
understand their business. Speak founder to owner, not corporate advertisement to buyer.

What they do not know: what Verity is, what to ask for, what a "system" would even look like.
What they respond to: their own mess described accurately, a concrete deliverable, no commitment.

## The 4-question test (every ad, first 3 seconds)

1. What are you selling? A business system built around how the business works.
2. Who is it for? A business owner running on Excel, WhatsApp and calls.
3. Why should I care? Software should follow my process, not the reverse.
4. What do I do next? Claim the free Business Blueprint.

If a cold viewer cannot answer all four from the ad alone, it is a brand film, not a performance ad.

## Funnel

```text
Meta ad (video / static)
  -> Lead form (claim the Business Blueprint)
  -> Sales qualification   "You requested a Verity Business Blueprint. Before we prepare it, I'd like to understand a little about your business."
  -> Product Manager discovery
  -> Business Blueprint
  -> Proposed Verity system
  -> Demo / validation
  -> Approval
  -> Implementation
  -> Client
```

The ad does not reveal the module catalogue. It sells outcome and process; Sales and PM qualify and explain depth.

## Content tiers

| Tier | Length | Job | Audience | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Performance | 15-30 s | Lead generation. Problem, Verity, why different, offer, CTA | Cold | `reels/R01-R06`, statics |
| 2 Consideration | 30-45 s | Explain the process: discovery, mapping, blueprint, configuration, demo, implementation | Retargeting (video viewers, site visitors) | Added after Tier 1 data |
| 3 Brand film | 30-60 s | Premium and credible, not a direct lead driver | Warm / organic | Existing `video/renders/films`, `trailer` |

Build order: Tier 1 first (6 reels, 3 hooks each, plus the first 6 statics). Tier 2 only when Tier 1 gives a winner.

## The message handoff

Ad: "Before you buy software, understand what your business needs." Sales: "Let's understand your business."
PM: "Here's what we found." Verity: "Here's what we propose." Each step must sound like the previous one promised it.
