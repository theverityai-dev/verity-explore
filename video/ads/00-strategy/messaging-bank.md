## Mechanism line (shown, not just said)

`Your workflows → Your requirements → Verity blueprint → Your system`

## Differentiator lines

- We understand your business first. Then configure the system around it.
- We don't start with a demo. We start by understanding your business.
- Not 50 modules to choose from. Tell us how your business works; we figure out what the software should be.

## Offer lines

- ₹5,000 Business Blueprint™ · FREE for a limited time (final wording per `offer-and-claims.md` D1)
- We'll analyse how your business works and show you what your ideal business system could look like.

## CTAs

Strong: Get Your Free Business Blueprint → · Claim Your Free Blueprint → · Get Yours Free → · Request My Blueprint →
Closing VO: "Tell us how your business works. We'll show you what your system could look like."

## Banned

- CTAs: "Learn more", "Contact us", "Book a consultation" (it sells a sales call, not a deliverable).
- In first-batch creatives: ERP, CRM, AI, "modules", "123 industries", feature catalogues. (R03 uses "ERP" and "modules"
  deliberately as the contrast; that is the one exception, and it is a hook, not a feature list.)
- Visuals: stock photos, smiling businessmen, handshakes, "AI futuristic" graphics, glowing brains, neon.
- Claims: guarantees, savings percentages, fake testimonials, client logos we do not have, countdown timers with no end date.

## Tone

Plain, direct, a little sharp. A founder talking to a founder. Short sentences. Hinglish in VO where the brief says so;
on-screen headline text in English, captions in Roman-script Hinglish.
