# <ID> treatment

Written before any animation. A shot is not animated until its keyframe still scores 8 or more on every line of the
art-direction gate (bottom of this file).

## Creative concept (one paragraph)

The visual idea, the world it happens in, and the single metaphor that carries the film. Not a list of scenes.

## World

- Space: <architecture, horizon, scale>
- Materials: <frosted acrylic, matte drafting film, paper, stone, aluminium; which, and where>
- Lighting: <key direction and quality, shadows, bounce>
- Lens and camera: <focal length equivalent, the one continuous move, parallax rule>
- Typography: <sizes, where text lives, what stays static>
- Accent: Verity blue only for <paths, state, CTA>
- Avoid: cards flying in, generic SaaS diagrams, gradients, glow, particles, elastic easing, UI popping from nowhere, kinetic-text gimmicks

## Shots

Copy one block per shot. Times come from the recorded VO (`vo/words.json`), never invented.

```text
SHOT <n>  <t0>-<t1> s   VO: "<exact line>"

Purpose:        <what the viewer must understand>
Visual concept: <the image, in one or two sentences>
Composition:    <subject size and position, where the headline sits, negative space, focal point>
Camera:         <lens equivalent, move, speed, origin>
Materials:      <what the objects are made of>
Lighting:       <key, shadows, bounce>
Motion:         <what moves, how far, which ease; what stays still>
Typography:     <exact copy, size, anchor; or "none">
Avoid:          <shot-specific traps>
```
