5 timed scripts

Short-form scripts

Timings are a guide for a cut of about a minute. Each runs in the same order: the problem, the free seven-day demo offer, the change, the owner view, no charge if it does not fit.

Script 1

Document set

  1. 0 to 5s

    Shipping documents bank mein reject hue. Ek field galat thi.

  2. 5 to 15s

    Invoice mein ek quantity, packing list mein doosri, aur cargo ruka hua.

  3. 15 to 25s

    Aap apna business process batayiye. Hum saat din mein, aapke actual business data par, ek customised demo banate hain. Bilkul free.

  4. 25 to 40s

    Har shipment ka document checklist. Files juda hua, aur jo fields match honi chahiye unki jaanch.

  5. 40 to 50s

    Galti bank se pehle pakdi jaati hai.

  6. 50 to 60s

    Aapki team dekhe, pasand aaye tabhi aage badhiye. Nahi toh koi charge nahi.

Script 2

Realisation ki deadline

  1. 0 to 5s

    Export payment ki deadline chup chaap nikal gayi.

  2. 5 to 15s

    Kisi ne gina nahi tha aur buyer ne bhi yaad nahi dilaya.

  3. 15 to 25s

    Apne invoice dijiye. Saat din mein free demo.

  4. 25 to 40s

    Har invoice mein shipment ki tarikh, kitna aaya aur kitne din baaki. Limit ke paas wale upar.

  5. 40 to 50s

    Chase usi person ko jaata hai jo buyer ko jaanta hai.

  6. 50 to 60s

    Pasand aaye tabhi aage. Nahi toh koi charge nahi.

Script 3

Incentive ka paisa

  1. 0 to 5s

  2. 5 to 15s

  4. 25 to 40s

  5. 40 to 50s

    Aapka paisa aapke follow up se aata hai.

  6. 50 to 60s

Sales kit

Reaching and talking to exporters

Lead with: Rejected document sets and unrealised payments

Who to reach

Titles to search
Managing Director · Owner · Head of Exports · Export Manager · Director Finance · Head of Merchandising · General Manager Operations
Who decides
The owner or managing director decides. The export head and finance head influence.
Best business size
An export business with 20 to 250 people, a few large buyers and many shipments a month.

First message

Hello, I work with exporters on rejected document sets and payments that pass their deadline. We build a free seven day demo on your own shipment data. How do you check documents before they reach the bank?

Discovery questions

  1. How do you check that documents match before presentation?
  2. How do you track the deadline to realise payment?
  3. How do you keep up with incentive claims?
  4. How do you answer buyers asking for shipment status?
  5. How do you cost an order before quoting?
  6. How do you track claims and credit notes?

Objections

Our forwarder handles documents
The forwarder prepares them. We give you a check across all parties so you are not relying on one.
We use Tally
We keep Tally for accounts and add the shipment and document record.
Small team
The four week plan needs about 8 to 14 owner hours in total.
Cost
Cost depends on scope. We start with the one problem that costs most, and the demo is free.

Demo and proof

Demo to build
A shipment with a document mismatch flagged, invoices with days left to realise, an incentive claim past due and a cost sheet against actual.
Proof of value
Take the last five shipments and show unrealised payments and claim status.

Follow-up message

Thank you for your time. I have built the demo around the shipment you described, with its documents and realisation dates. When can your export head and I go through it for 20 minutes?
