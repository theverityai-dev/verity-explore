5 timed scripts

Short-form scripts

Timings are a guide for a cut of about a minute. Each runs in the same order: the problem, the free seven-day demo offer, the change, the owner view, no charge if it does not fit.

Script 1

Bill kab banega

  1. 0 to 5s

    Kaam ho gaya, bill abhi tak nahi bana.

  2. 5 to 15s

    Measurement book ek jagah, certification doosri jagah, aur office ko mahine ke end mein pata chalta hai.

  3. 15 to 25s

    Aap apna business process batayiye. Hum saat din mein, aapke actual business data par, ek customised demo banate hain. Bilkul free.

  4. 25 to 40s

    Contract item ka quantity aur rate. Site kaam darj karti hai, consultant certify karta hai, aur agla running bill us se bankar tayyar.

  5. 40 to 50s

    Retention aur deduction bhi har bill mein dikhte hain.

  6. 50 to 60s

    Aapki team dekhe, pasand aaye tabhi aage badhiye. Nahi toh koi charge nahi.

  3. 15 to 25s

Sales kit

Reaching and talking to construction companies

Lead with: Late running bills, retention stuck with clients and unchecked subcontractor payments

Who to reach

Titles to search
Managing Director · Owner · Director · Project Director · General Manager Projects · Head of Billing · Head of Accounts
Who decides
The owner or managing director decides. The project director and accounts head influence.
Best business size
A construction company with 50 to 500 people, several sites, subcontractors and running bills to clients.

First message

Hello, I work with construction companies on late running bills and money stuck in retention and unchecked subcontractor bills. We build a free seven day demo on one of your live projects. How do you know today what is certified but not yet billed?

Discovery questions

  1. How do you know what work is certified but not billed?
  2. How do you track retention and release dates?
  3. How do you price and track variations?
  4. How are subcontractor bills checked against measured work?
  5. How do you compare material issued with work done?
  6. How do you see profit while a project runs?

Objections

We use Tally and Excel
We keep them. We add the measurement, certification, subcontractor and material records they do not hold.
Site staff will not fill reports
We keep daily reports short and tied to what they already write.
We have an ERP
We connect the bill, variation and site records that ERPs seldom capture well on site.
Cost
Cost depends on scope. We start with the one project that costs you most, and the demo is free.

Demo and proof

Demo to build
A project with certified quantities building a running bill, retention held with release dates, a variation with approval and a subcontractor bill compared with measurement.
Proof of value
Take one live project and show certified but unbilled work and retention due.

Follow-up message

Thank you for your time. I have built the demo around the project you described, with its running bill and retention. When can your project director and I go through it for 20 minutes?
