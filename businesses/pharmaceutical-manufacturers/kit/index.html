  3. 15 to 25s

    Aap apna business process batayiye. Hum saat din mein, aapke actual business data par, ek customised demo banate hain. Bilkul free.

  4. 25 to 40s

Sales kit

Reaching and talking to pharmaceutical manufacturers

Lead with: Incomplete batch records and open deviations ahead of audits and the revised Schedule M

Who to reach

Titles to search
Managing Director · Owner · Head of Quality Assurance · Head of Production · Plant Head · Head of Quality Control · Director Operations
Who decides
The owner or managing director decides. The head of quality and plant head influence strongly.
Best business size
A formulation or contract pharma plant with 50 to 300 people, several products and customers who audit.

First message

Hello, I work with pharma plants on one problem: incomplete batch records and deviations that stay open. We build a free seven day demo on your own batch and deviation data. How do you catch a missed entry before review today?

Discovery questions

  1. How do you catch a missed batch record entry?
  2. How is material status controlled at issue?
  3. How do QC results reach the batch record?
  4. How do you follow an out-of-specification investigation?
  5. How are deviations and changes tracked by age?
  6. How ready are you for the revised Schedule M?

Objections

We use paper and it is validated
We do not ask you to drop what works. We start with the records that are hardest to track, such as deviations and training, and show the record on your data.
Regulatory risk of software
Verity records and tracks. You keep your procedures and approvals, and we map the software around them.
Our team is overloaded
The four week plan needs about 8 to 14 owner hours in total, and we do the rest with your team.
Cost
Cost depends on scope. We start with the one problem that costs most, and the demo is free.

Demo and proof

Demo to build
One batch record with a missed step flagged, a lot blocked until QC approval, a deviation list by age and a recall drill from complaint to customer list.
Proof of value
Take one open deviation or one recent complaint and show its full linked record.

Follow-up message

Thank you for your time. I have built the demo around the deviation you described, with its batch and its history. When can your head of quality and I go through it for 20 minutes?
