Sales kit

Reaching and talking to textile manufacturers

Lead with: Complaints that cannot be traced, and margin lost when fibre prices move

Who to reach

Titles to search
Managing Director · Owner · Director Operations · Mill Manager · General Manager Production · Head of Marketing · Head of Quality
Who decides
The owner or managing director decides. The mill manager and quality head influence, and accounts raises cost and credit questions.
Best business size
A spinning or weaving mill with 50 to 500 people, one to three units, and buyers that pay through brokers.

First message

Hello, I work with textile mills on one problem: complaints that cannot be traced to a cotton lot, and margins that move when fibre prices do. We build a free seven day demo on your own lot and order data. How do you trace a yarn complaint today?

Discovery questions

  1. How do you trace a yarn or cloth complaint back to the cotton lot?
  2. How do you know the yield of a mixing while it is running?
  3. How is loom or frame efficiency recorded and who sees it?
  4. What happens when cotton prices move after you fix an order price?
  5. How do you track cloth that goes to processors?
  6. Who follows up buyer payments and how are promises recorded?

Objections

We are used to our register system
We keep the registers you trust and connect them to one lot record, so tracing does not depend on one person's memory.
Our people are not computer literate
We start with a few short screens each shift in charge can fill in a minute, in your own terms.
ERP failed for us earlier
Those usually force the mill to fit the software. We map your process first and show the demo on your data before you decide.
It will be too costly
Cost depends on scope. We start with the one problem that costs most, and the demo is free.

Demo and proof

Demo to build
A mixing with its cotton lots, one yarn complaint traced back, a processor return with a shortage and an order flagged for a cotton rate move, built from the mill's own lot sheets.
Proof of value
Trace one real complaint from last quarter to its bales and show the yield of that mixing.

Follow-up message

Thank you for your time. I have built the demo around the complaint you described, from cotton lot to yarn. When can your mill manager and I go through it for 20 minutes?
