Short-form scripts

Timings are a guide for a cut of about a minute. Each runs in the same order: the problem, the free seven-day demo offer, the change, the owner view, no charge if it does not fit.

    Udhaar badhta gaya aur limit kisne dekhi?

    Munim ne order le liya, owner ko baad mein pata chala.

  3. 15 to 25s

    Aap apna business process batayiye. Hum saat din mein, aapke actual business data par, ek customised demo banate hain. Bilkul free.

  4. 25 to 40s

    Har retailer ki limit aur period. Order us se upar jaye to ruk jata hai aur aap ek tap se faisla lete hain, baaki dues aur age ke saath.

  5. 40 to 50s

    Aapki team dekhe, pasand aaye tabhi aage badhiye. Nahi toh koi charge nahi.

Sales kit

Reaching and talking to wholesalers

Lead with: Udhaar and collections, and the margin lost when floors are broken

Who to reach

Titles to search
Owner · Managing Director · Partner · Director · Head of Sales · Accounts Head · General Manager
Who decides
The owner decides, often with a partner or the munim in the loop. The accounts head influences credit and collection decisions.
Best business size
A wholesale business with 15 to 100 people, hundreds of retailers on credit and several suppliers.

First message

Hello, I work with wholesalers on credit limits, collections and slow stock. We build a free seven day demo on your own retailer and stock data. How do you decide if a retailer can take more udhaar today?

Discovery questions

  1. How do you check a retailer's limit when an order comes in?
  2. Who follows up outstanding and how is the promise recorded?
  3. How do you track post-dated cheques?
  4. How do you set and protect the floor rate for each line?
  5. How do you know which stock has been sitting longest?
  6. Which lines and retailers make you the most margin?

Objections

Our munim handles everything and it works
We keep the munim's way and make his records visible to you, so you do not depend on one person.
Retailers will not change how they order
They do not have to. We record orders as they come, in whatever channel.
Tally is enough
Tally records accounts. We add the credit, order, cheque and stock views that you check in the same day.
Cost
Cost depends on scope. We start with the problem that costs most, and the demo is free.

Demo and proof

Demo to build
A retailer over the credit limit waiting for approval, an ageing list with follow-up tasks, a cheque deposit list and slow stock by age, built from the wholesaler's own sheets.
Proof of value
Take the top twenty retailers and show their outstanding age and the stock that has sat longest.

Follow-up message

Thank you for your time. I have built the demo around the retailers you described, with their outstanding and limits. When can you and your munim go through it for 20 minutes?
