5 timed scripts

Short-form scripts

Timings are a guide for a cut of about a minute. Each runs in the same order: the problem, the free seven-day demo offer, the change, the owner view, no charge if it does not fit.

Script 1

Purani drawing

  1. 0 to 5s

    Customer ne drawing approve ki thi. Floor par purani drawing chal rahi hai.

  2. 5 to 15s

    Phone par badlav hua, sketch par likha, aur head carpenter ke paas purana print.

  3. 15 to 25s

    Aap apna business process batayiye. Hum saat din mein, aapke actual business data par, ek customised demo banate hain. Bilkul free.

  4. 25 to 40s

    Har order mein drawing ke versions, kisne badlav maanga, aur approval. Floor ko sirf latest approved dikhti hai.

  5. 40 to 50s

    Dispute ho to tareekh ke saath jawab milta hai.

  6. 50 to 60s

    Aapki team dekhe, pasand aaye tabhi aage badhiye. Nahi toh koi charge nahi.

Sales kit

Reaching and talking to furniture manufacturers

Lead with: Rework from drawing changes and board waste

Who to reach

Titles to search
Owner · Managing Director · Director · Works Manager · Head of Production · Head Carpenter · Operations Manager
Who decides
The owner decides. The works manager and head carpenter influence, and the designer influences drawing control.
Best business size
A workshop or factory with 15 to 150 people, custom or semi-custom orders, and a showroom or dealer channel.

First message

Hello, I work with furniture makers on one problem: jobs rebuilt because the drawing changed or the fittings were missing. We build a free seven day demo on your own orders so your team can see it. How do you track drawing changes today?

Discovery questions

  1. How do you make sure the floor works from the approved drawing?
  2. How do you cost a custom order and compare it afterwards?
  3. How much board waste do you see and where do offcuts go?
  4. How do you track jobs through the stages?
  5. How do you plan delivery and installation?
  6. How do you handle after-sales requests?

Objections

We are a workshop, not a company that uses software
We keep the routine your carpenters know and record only what you need to see.
Our head carpenter will resist
We build the screens with the head carpenter and show that it reduces the calls he has to answer.
Too expensive
Cost depends on scope. We start with the one problem that costs you most, and the demo is free.
Customers change things all the time
That is exactly why drawing versions and approvals are on the order.

Demo and proof

Demo to build
An order with three drawing versions, a board plan with an offcut returned, a fittings shortfall and a delivery held until the site is ready, built from the workshop's own jobs.
Proof of value
Take one reworked job from last quarter and show how the order record would have caught it.

Follow-up message

Thank you for your time. I have built the demo around the order with the drawing change you described. When can your head carpenter and I go through it for 20 minutes?
