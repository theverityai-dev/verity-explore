26 reel hooks

Opening lines

One line each. Pick the one that matches the problem on screen.

  • Buyer ne size ratio badal diya. Cutting table ko kisne bataya?

  • Trims nahi aaye aur line aadhi pe ruki hai. Ye kal hi pata chal sakta tha.

  • Sampling mein do hafte nikal gaye. Ship date wahi hai. Kaun jawab dega?

  • Line ki efficiency shaam ko pata chalti hai. Tab tak din nikal chuka hota hai.

  • Fabric invoice se 3 percent kam aaya. Cutting table pe jaakar pata chala.

  • 800 extra pieces cut ho gaye. Order mein nahi the.

  • Embroidery wale ko 2000 pieces bheje. Wapas kitne aaye, sahi hisaab hai?

  • Ship date se pehle air freight ka kharcha. Ye bacha ja sakta tha.

  • Costing sheet mein margin dikhta hai. Order ke baad actual margin kaun dekhta hai?

  • Defect final inspection mein mila. Line pe kab ban raha tha, kisko pata?

  • Naya style aaya aur line ki output aadhi. Is learning curve ka paisa kisne gina?

  • Roz subah unit heads ko phone karke poochna padta hai kya chal raha hai?

  • Operator absent hua aur aapko line pe pata chala. Kal shaam ko pata ho sakta tha.

  • Export payment due thi 40 din pehle. Kisi ne yaad dilaya?

  • Buyer ka revision email aaya. Merchandiser chutti pe tha.

  • Fabric lot ka shade alag nikla. Kis supplier ka, kis roll ka, koi record hai?

  • Incentive claim file karna bhool gaye. Paisa gaya.

  • Do unit, teen buyer, ek Excel sheet. Kaun sa order khatre mein hai?

  • Wage dispute pe teen log, ek dopahar. Attendance aur output ek jagah kyun nahi?

  • Aap factory mein nahi hote to line check kaun karta hai?

  • Trim ka supplier bola kal bhej dunga. Kal kab aata hai?

  • Rework ka kharcha kisi ko dikhta hai? Kaun si operation se aa raha hai?

  • Cutting ke baad pata chala ki fabric kam hai. Isko receipt pe pakadna tha.

  • Sample approve nahi hua aur bulk fabric ruka hai. Owner kaun?

  • Buyer bola late shipment ka discount do. Kahan atka tha, kisko pata?

  • Packing list aur cartons mein farq nikla. Buyer ke warehouse mein pata chala.

Sales kit

Reaching and talking to garment manufacturers

Lead with: Late shipments from revisions, trim gaps and slow sampling

Who to reach

Titles to search
Managing Director · Owner · Director Operations · General Manager Production · Head of Merchandising · Factory Manager · Head of Planning
Who decides
The owner or managing director decides. The general manager or merchandising head influences, and accounts raises cost questions.
Best business size
Roughly 100 to 500 machines or workers, with several buyers and at least one line of outside job work, where the merchandiser is the link between everything.

First message

Hello, I work with garment makers on one problem: buyer changes and trim gaps that show up at the cutting table or on the line. We build a free seven day demo on your actual order data so your team can see it. Can I ask how you track revisions today?

Discovery questions

  1. How does a buyer revision reach the cutting table today?
  2. Which was the last order that went to air freight, and what step caused it?
  3. How do you know on Tuesday that the Friday shipment is safe?
  4. Who tracks trims and labels, and where is that list?
  5. What do you do when job work returns short?
  6. How do you cost an order after it ships?
  7. Which numbers do you ask your unit heads for every morning?

Objections

We already use Excel and it works
Excel works until the person who maintains it is away. We keep the sheets you trust and connect them to one order record, so the sheet stops being the only copy.
My team will not use new software
We start with the three or four screens each role needs and bring your own formats. Your supervisors keep their routine; Verity records it.
We tried an ERP and it failed
Most fail because the factory is asked to fit the software. We map your actual process first and configure around it. You see the demo on your data before you decide.
It is too expensive
Cost depends on scope, so we start with the one problem that costs you most. The demo is free and you decide after seeing it.
We cannot spare time for a project
The fixed four week plan needs about 8 to 14 owner hours in total. The rest is done by us with your team.

Demo and proof

Demo to build
An order with two revisions, a trim shortfall dated to the line and a job work challan with a shortage, loaded from the buyer's real order sheet.
Proof of value
Take one at-risk order and show, with your own data, the step that is behind and by how many days.

Follow-up message

Thank you for your time. I have put together the demo around the order you described, including the revision and the trim gap. When can your merchandising head and I walk through it for 20 minutes?
