Sales kit

Reaching and talking to food manufacturers

Lead with: Batch traceability, holds that hold, and returns from short shelf life

Who to reach

Titles to search
Managing Director · Owner · Plant Head · Head of Quality · Head of Production · Head of Supply Chain · General Manager Operations
Who decides
The owner or managing director decides. The plant head and quality head influence, and sales raises shelf-life and returns questions.
Best business size
A packaged food or beverage maker with 30 to 300 people, several products and distributors or retailers who audit or return stock.

First message

Hello, I work with food manufacturers on batch traceability and returns from short shelf life. We build a free seven day demo on your own batch and stock data. How long does a recall drill take you today?

Discovery questions

  1. How do you find every batch an ingredient lot went into?
  2. How do you pick stock by best-before date?
  3. What happens when a check fails during packing?
  4. How are holds communicated and enforced?
  5. How are returns recorded and linked to the batch?
  6. How do you track licences, tests and hygiene checks?

Objections

We already have ERP for accounts
We do not replace accounts. We connect batch, lot and hold records that accounts software does not hold.
Operators will not use screens
We start with the few screens each line leader needs and use your language and terms.
Cost
Cost depends on scope. We start with the problem that costs most, and the demo is free.
Our process is changing
We configure around how you work now, and change it with you as it moves.

Demo and proof

Demo to build
A lot traced to its batches and customers, a failed check blocking packing, a hold that stops dispatch, and first-expiry picking, built from the factory's own sheets.
Proof of value
Run a recall drill on one real lot and show how long it takes with the record.

Follow-up message

Thank you for your time. I have built the demo around the lot you described, from receipt to customer. When can your quality head and I go through it for 20 minutes?
