5 timed scripts

Short-form scripts

Timings are a guide for a cut of about a minute. Each runs in the same order: the problem, the free seven-day demo offer, the change, the owner view, no charge if it does not fit.

Script 1

Stage ka demand

  1. 0 to 5s

    Stage ho gaya par demand letter nahi gaya.

  2. 5 to 15s

    Engineer ne bola, accounts ko pata nahi, aur paisa hafte late.

  3. 15 to 25s

    Aap apna business process batayiye. Hum saat din mein, aapke actual business data par, ek customised demo banate hain. Bilkul free.

  4. 25 to 40s

    Har ghar ka payment schedule construction stage se juda. Engineer photo ke saath stage mark karta hai aur demand tayyar.

  5. 40 to 50s

    Customer ke due date aur calls ka record bhi.

  6. 50 to 60s

    Aapki team dekhe, pasand aaye tabhi aage badhiye. Nahi toh koi charge nahi.

Script 2

Bank ka paisa

  1. 0 to 5s

    Bank se disbursement ka status phone se pooch rahe hain.

  2. 5 to 15s

    Stage ho gaya, certificate gaya, par paisa kab aayega kisi ko nahi pata.

  3. 15 to 25s

    Paanch loan dijiye. Saat din mein free demo.

  4. 25 to 40s

    Har ghar ka bank, sanctioned amount, stage aur documents. Stage ho gaya aur disbursement nahi hua to flag.

  5. 40 to 50s

    Accounts ko pata hai kise phone karna hai.

  6. 50 to 60s

    Pasand aaye tabhi aage. Nahi toh koi charge nahi.

Script 3

Extra ka paisa

  1. 0 to 5s

    Customer ne change manga par paise nahi liye.

  2. 5 to 15s

    Sales ne haan kar di, site ne kar diya, accounts ko pata hi nahi.

  3. 15 to 25s

    Ek change dijiye. Saat din mein free demo.

  4. 25 to 40s

    Har change ek record: maang, estimate, customer ki approval aur wo stage jiske baad change nahi ho sakta.

  5. 40 to 50s

    Extra ka bill sahi samay par.

  6. 50 to 60s

    Demo pasand aaye tabhi aage badhiye. Nahi toh koi charge nahi.

Script 4

Possession ki date

  1. 0 to 5s

    Possession ki date aayi aur ghar taiyaar nahi.

  2. 5 to 15s

    Ek hafte pehle pata chala, customer ko kya bataenge?

  4. 25 to 40s

  5. 40 to 50s

    Customer se baat pehle hoti hai.

  6. 50 to 60s

    Pasand aaye tabhi aage. Nahi toh koi charge nahi.

Owner ka view

Sales kit

Reaching and talking to home builders

Lead with: Demands raised late, change requests not billed and possession dates at risk

Who to reach

Titles to search
Managing Director · Owner · Director · Project Head · General Manager Sales · Head of Accounts · Head of Customer Relations
Who decides
The owner or managing director decides. The project head and accounts head influence.
Best business size
A residential builder with 30 to 300 people, one to five projects and buyers on stage-linked payment plans with bank loans.

First message

Hello, I work with home builders on demands that go out late and extras that are never billed. We build a free seven day demo on one of your projects. How do you know the day a stage is reached that a demand is due?

Discovery questions

  1. How do you link demands to construction stages?
  2. How do you track loan disbursements?
  3. How do you handle customer change requests and their cost?
  4. How do you know a home's possession date is at risk?
  5. How do you track snags and post-handover defects?
  6. How do you see cost per home?

Objections

We use project accounting software
We keep it and add the stage, change and snag records that sit outside it.
Site engineers are busy
We keep the stage update to a photo and a tick.
Cost
Cost depends on scope. We start with one project, and the demo is free.
Busy launch period
The four week plan needs about 8 to 14 owner hours in total.

Demo and proof

Demo to build
A home with a stage reached and a demand ready, a loan stage not yet disbursed, a change request with approval and a possession date at risk.
Proof of value
Take one project and show demands that were late and extras not billed.

Follow-up message

Thank you for your time. I have built the demo around the project you described, from stage to demand. When can your project head and I go through it for 20 minutes?
