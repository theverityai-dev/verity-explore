5 timed scripts

Short-form scripts

Timings are a guide for a cut of about a minute. Each runs in the same order: the problem, the free seven-day demo offer, the change, the owner view, no charge if it does not fit.

Script 1

Container kahan hai

  1. 0 to 5s

    Container kahan hai, har baar forwarder ko phone karna padta hai?

  2. 5 to 15s

    Forwarder ek baat, clearing agent doosri, supplier teesri.

  3. 15 to 25s

    Aap apna business process batayiye. Hum saat din mein, aapke actual business data par, ek customised demo banate hain. Bilkul free.

  4. 25 to 40s

    Har shipment ek record: supplier, order, vessel, container, dates. Ek board par stage ke hisaab se.

  5. 40 to 50s

    Aapko poochna nahi padta.

  6. 50 to 60s

    Aapki team dekhe, pasand aaye tabhi aage badhiye. Nahi toh koi charge nahi.

Sales kit

Reaching and talking to importers

Lead with: Landed cost known only after the sale, and demurrage from untracked free days

Who to reach

Titles to search
Managing Director · Owner · Head of Import · Head of Purchase · Director Finance · Head of Logistics · General Manager Operations
Who decides
The owner or managing director decides. The finance head and head of import influence.
Best business size
A trading or manufacturing business with 20 to 200 people and a regular flow of imported containers from several suppliers.

First message

Hello, I work with importers on landed cost and container tracking. We build a free seven day demo on your own shipment data. How do you know the real cost of a shipment today?

Discovery questions

  1. How do you track where each container is?
  2. How do you catch a document mismatch before arrival?
  3. Who watches free days and container return dates?
  4. When do you know the landed cost per unit?
  5. How are advances to suppliers followed up?
  6. How do you track exchange rate exposure on open orders?

Objections

The clearing agent handles this
The agent does clearance. We give you one view across all the parties so you do not have to ask each of them.
We use Tally
We keep Tally for accounts and add the shipment and landed cost record it does not hold.
Not enough staff for a project
The four week plan needs about 8 to 14 owner hours in total.
Cost
Cost depends on scope. We start with the one problem that costs most, and the demo is free.

Demo and proof

Demo to build
A board of shipments by stage, a document mismatch flagged, a container with days left and a landed cost per unit, built from the importer's own shipments.
Proof of value
Take the last three shipments and show their real landed cost and any free days lost.

Follow-up message

Thank you for your time. I have built the demo around the shipment you described, with its landed cost. When can your finance head and I go through it for 20 minutes?
