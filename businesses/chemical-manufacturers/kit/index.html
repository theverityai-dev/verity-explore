Sales kit

Reaching and talking to chemical manufacturers

Lead with: Batch traceability and certificates that are right the first time

Who to reach

Titles to search
Managing Director · Owner · Plant Head · Head of Quality · Head of Production · Head of Compliance · General Manager Operations
Who decides
The owner or managing director decides. The plant head and quality head influence, and compliance raises record questions.
Best business size
A batch chemical or specialty chemical plant with 30 to 300 people and customers who ask for certificates and traceability.

First message

Hello, I work with chemical manufacturers on batch traceability and certificates. We build a free seven day demo on your own batch and lot data. How do you trace a complaint to its raw material lot today?

Discovery questions

  1. How do you link a raw material lot to the batches it went into?
  2. How is yield tracked per batch and who sees it?
  3. How is a certificate of analysis prepared and released?
  4. How do you find a retained sample for a complaint?
  5. How are licence and consent dates tracked?
  6. How do you record effluent and waste data?

Objections

We have a lab system
We connect your batch and lot records to the lab results so the certificate and traceability come from one record.
Compliance is handled by a consultant
We keep the consultant's role and give them clean records to work from.
Our process is too complex
We start with one product line and add more after you see it working.
Cost
Cost depends on scope. We start with the problem that costs most, and the demo is free.

Demo and proof

Demo to build
A batch with its lots, a yield flag, a certificate drawn from lab results with release recorded, and a complaint traced to a retained sample, built from the plant's own records.
Proof of value
Trace one real complaint from last year to its batch, lots and retained sample.

Follow-up message

Thank you for your time. I have built the demo around the complaint you described, from batch to raw material lot. When can your quality head and I go through it for 20 minutes?
