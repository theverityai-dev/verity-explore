5 timed scripts

Short-form scripts

Timings are a guide for a cut of about a minute. Each runs in the same order: the problem, the free seven-day demo offer, the change, the owner view, no charge if it does not fit.

Script 1

Quote aur asli cost

  1. 0 to 5s

    Quote feel pe bana tha. Job ke baad asli cost kisne dekha?

  2. 5 to 15s

    Job nikal gayi, margin ka andaaza raha.

  3. 15 to 25s

    Aap apna business process batayiye. Hum saat din mein, aapke actual business data par, ek customised demo banate hain. Bilkul free.

  4. 25 to 40s

    Quote spec, material rate, waste aur tooling se banta hai. Job band hone par quote aur asli cost saath dikhte hain.

  5. 40 to 50s

    Agla quote asli cost se shuru hota hai.

  6. 50 to 60s

    Aapki team dekhe, pasand aaye tabhi aage badhiye. Nahi toh koi charge nahi.

Sales kit

Reaching and talking to packaging companies

Lead with: Quotes that never get compared with real cost, and wastage no one can explain

Who to reach

Titles to search
Managing Director · Owner · Director · Plant Head · General Manager Operations · Head of Production · Head of Costing
Who decides
The owner or managing director decides. The plant head and estimator influence.
Best business size
A corrugated, carton, label or flexible packaging unit with 30 to 250 people, many small repeat jobs and customer-owned tooling.

First message

Hello, I work with packaging companies on quotes that are never compared with real cost, and on wastage that has no cause recorded. We build a free seven day demo on your own jobs. How do you check a job's margin after it closes?

Discovery questions

  1. How do you build and check a quote?
  2. How do you track waste by cause?
  3. How do you find a die or plate when a repeat order comes?
  4. How is tooling cost recovered from customers?
  5. How do you control the approved artwork?
  6. How do you trace a complaint to a reel or a shift?

Objections

We have a job costing sheet
We keep it and connect it to the job record so the actual numbers come from the floor, not typed again.
Operators will not fill screens
We keep entries short and in the terms your operators use.
Cost
Cost depends on scope. We start with the problem that costs most, and the demo is free.
We are busy with orders
The four week plan needs about 8 to 14 owner hours in total.

Demo and proof

Demo to build
A closed job with quote against actual, waste by reason, a tool record with unrecovered cost, and a complaint traced to a reel, built from your own jobs.
Proof of value
Take five closed jobs and show real margin against the quote.

Follow-up message

Thank you for your time. I have built the demo around the job you described, quote against actual. When can your plant head and I go through it for 20 minutes?
