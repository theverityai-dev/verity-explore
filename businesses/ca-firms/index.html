How it runs today

Most CA firms manage a large and strict calendar with a whiteboard and a good memory.

The work has a shape. Every month, every quarter, every year, the same filings return, each with a due date set by the government and not by you. What changes is the client mix, the staff and the notices that arrive without warning.

The calendar is usually a sheet kept by a manager. Documents come through WhatsApp, email and couriers. Working papers sit on staff laptops. Fees are billed after the busy season, when someone has time. The partner signs, and holds the risk for all of it.

The profession is large and competitive. As of February 2025, there were about 4.08 lakh ICAI members, of whom about 1.6 lakh held a certificate of practice, and nearly 99,000 registered firms. Clients can compare, and expect fast, accurate, responsive service.

What most CA firms run on

  • A manager's due date sheet or wall calendar
  • Tally, GST and income tax software
  • WhatsApp groups per client
  • Shared drives and staff laptops for working papers
  • Excel for fees and outstanding

Each tool does its own job. None of them tell the partner, before the last week, that a filing is about to be late.

Where a CA firm actually loses time, money and sleep.

These are the situations partners and managers of CA practices describe again and again. For each one: what it looks like on an ordinary day, what it costs, and what Verity does about it.

  1. 01Compliance

    A compliance calendar that depends on one person

    What it looks like

    The manager who keeps the due date sheet is on leave in the week of the quarterly TDS returns. The sheet is on her laptop, and the dates for new clients were never added.

    What it costs you

    Late fees and interest fall on the client and the blame falls on the firm. For monthly GST returns, the late fee is generally ₹50 a day for returns with tax, subject to caps.

    How Verity fixes it

    Each client has a record of its registrations and filing frequency, and every due date follows from it. Verity shows what is due this week across all clients, who is preparing and who is reviewing, and it does not depend on any one laptop. Your team files on the government portals; Verity keeps the plan and the status.

  2. 02Compliance

    Rule changes that nobody applies to each client

    What it looks like

    The Income-tax Act, 2025 came into force on 1 April 2026, while FY 2025-26 income is generally still assessed under the 1961 Act. A new return form, a changed threshold or a new portal advisory arrives, and the team asks which clients it touches.

    What it costs you

    Work done under the wrong assumption, rework on filing day and clients who ask the firm questions it cannot quickly answer.

    How Verity fixes it

    A change is logged once with the clients it touches, such as by registration type, turnover band or entity type. Verity creates a review task for each affected client's lead, and the decision is written on the client's record.

    See the screen
  3. 03Clients

    Clients who send documents on the last day

    What it looks like

    The ITR or audit deadline is eight days away and a client's bank statements, loan certificates and TDS certificates are still missing. The client says "I thought it was ready."

    What it costs you

    The staff work through the night, quality drops and review gets squeezed. A vendor guide for CA firms says bills stack up and bank statements arrive late every month and puts the overtime at 10 to 15 days; that is a vendor claim, but the pattern is familiar.

    How Verity fixes it

    Every assignment has a document checklist built from the client's type. Verity shows what has arrived and what is missing for every client, orders them by due date, and creates a chase task for the owner. Your team sends the request; Verity tracks it.

    See the screen
  4. 04Clients

    WhatsApp as the only record of what was asked

    What it looks like

    A client says he sent the Form 16 on the 3rd. The staff member who handled it has left. Nobody can find the message.

    What it costs you

    Arguments with clients, repeated requests and no audit trail. An ICAI AI platform article names this exact pattern: a crowded WhatsApp channel and no record of what was sent, when and by whom.

    How Verity fixes it

    Requests and receipts are recorded against the client and the assignment, with the date, the document and the person. Anyone in the firm can see what was asked and what came back.

    See the screen
  5. 05Assignments

    Working papers and audit files on staff laptops

    What it looks like

    An audit assistant keeps the sampling, the confirmation letters and the ledger scrutiny in a folder on his laptop. He is moved to another client in the middle of the audit.

    What it costs you

    Lost evidence, repeated work and a review that cannot be done without the person who prepared the files.

    How Verity fixes it

    Each assignment is a record with stages: planning, fieldwork, review, report. Working papers attach to the stage, with who prepared and who reviewed them and when. A new person can pick the file up from the record.

  6. 06Assignments

    Review bottleneck at the partner

    What it looks like

    Forty returns and three audit reports are waiting for partner review in the last week of September. The partner reviews in the order they were handed over and not in the order of urgency.

    What it costs you

    Rushed reviews are where errors slip through, and every error carries the partner's signature.

    How Verity fixes it

    Each item waiting for review shows its due date, the reviewer and any open points. Verity sorts the review queue by deadline and risk, and shows the partner what must be reviewed first.

    See the screen
  7. 07Compliance

    Certificates and reports signed without a clean trail

    What it looks like

    A bank asks for a net worth certificate, a turnover certificate and a stock statement review on the same day. Each has to be checked, signed and logged.

    What it costs you

    ICAI made UDIN mandatory for practising CAs from 1 January 2019, so the partner has to generate a number for each certified document. A missed or mistaken UDIN is a professional conduct risk and a client embarrassment.

    How Verity fixes it

    Each certificate or attest assignment is a record with the document, the reviewer, the sign-off state and a field for the UDIN generated on ICAI's portal. Verity shows signed items without a UDIN so the gap is caught before the document leaves.

  8. 08Compliance

    Notices arrive and the reply date is missed

    What it looks like

    A client forwards a photo of a departmental notice a week after it arrived. The reply window has shortened. The notice was addressed to a registered mobile no one checks.

    What it costs you

    Ex parte orders, extra penalties and an urgent reply drafted in a day.

    How Verity fixes it

    A notice is a record against the client with the section, the authority, the date received and the reply date. Verity assigns it to a senior, lists notices by due date and shows the reply status. Your team replies on the portal.

    Article assistants and juniors overloaded or idle

    What it looks like

    One article assistant is on four audits and a tax matter, while another is learning slowly on a single client. Training depends on who the senior happens to be.

    What it costs you

    Burnout, mistakes and slow learning. The firm also loses the chance to see who is ready for more.

    How Verity fixes it

    Verity shows each person's assignments, due dates and logged hours side by side, and a training log shows who has worked on what. Partners assign new work from this view and rebalance before the crunch.

  10. 10Money

    Fees billed late and work done on assurances

    What it costs you

    How Verity fixes it

    Each assignment has its fee terms and invoice dates. Verity raises invoice tasks at the agreed stage, ages unpaid invoices by client and shows the partner the follow-up list with reminders noted. The firm bills and collects; Verity tracks it.

How we work with you

We set it up with you, in four weeks.

You do not get a login and a manual. We sit in your office through a real filing week, build Verity around how your clients and assignments move, bring your data in, then stay on.

    Discovery and mapping

    What we do
    Sit with a partner, a manager and two seniors during a busy filing week. Follow a client from engagement to filing to bill, and note where the dates, documents and reviews are held today.
    What we need from you

    What you have at the end

  2. Weeks 2 to 3

    Configuration

    What we do
    Set up client types and registrations, filing calendars, document checklists, assignment stages for audits and certificates, notice handling, time codes, engagement scopes, invoice schedules and permissions for partners, staff and clients.
    What we need from you
    Review the setup with a manager and two seniors. Tell us where a stage, a checklist or a review step is wrong.
    What you have at the end
    A working Verity with your calendars, checklists and fee terms, ready for your data.

  3. Weeks 3 to 4

    Migration and training

    What we do
    Bring in your clients, registrations, live assignments, open notices and unpaid fees from the sheets and files you use. Train partners, managers and article assistants at their desks.

    Your client sheet and due-date sheet in whatever form they exist, and a check of the live dates. An hour with each role.
    What you have at the end
    Your clients, dates and fees inside Verity, and a team that has run part of a filing week on it.

  4. After go-live

    Operations partnership

    What we do
    Stay on after launch. Review the first filing cycle with you, tune the calendars and reminders, and handle changes when you add clients, services or staff.
    What we need from you
    A short monthly review with a partner.
    What you have at the end
    A system that keeps matching how the practice runs, with someone responsible for it.

Rules and paperwork

The rules a CA firm has to keep up with.

Verity does not give tax, audit or legal advice and does not file anything. It keeps the calendar and the records these rules depend on in order, so your team prepares and the partner signs from a clear position.

  • Filing calendars across taxes

    What is expected

    GST, TDS, income tax and company filings each have their own dates, and the Income-tax Act, 2025 came into force on 1 April 2026 while FY 2025-26 income is generally still assessed under the 1961 Act. Monthly GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B are generally due on the 11th and 20th.

    How Verity helps

    Each client's filings and due dates follow from its registrations, with a preparer and a reviewer for each.

    Patron Accounting, 2026

  • UDIN for certified documents

    What is expected

    ICAI made UDIN mandatory for practising CAs from 1 January 2019. It is generated on ICAI's portal and lets banks and regulators verify a certified document. The scope by document type was phased in, so check ICAI's current directions.

    How Verity helps

    Certificate and attest assignments carry a UDIN field and cannot be marked ready without it.

    TaxGuru, 2018

  • E-invoicing and other thresholds

    What is expected

    E-invoicing generally applies where turnover exceeded ₹5 crore in any year since FY 2017-18, with a 30-day reporting limit for ₹10 crore and above from 1 April 2025.

    How Verity helps

    Turnover to date and key limits are shown on each client, with a review task as one is approached.

    TaxGuru, 2026

  • Data protection and confidentiality

    What is expected

    The Digital Personal Data Protection Rules were notified on 13 November 2025, with most duties due about 18 months later. Professional confidentiality duties apply separately under ICAI's code.

    How Verity helps

    Client documents carry role and client permissions, and views and downloads are logged.

    Mondaq, 2025

Rules, dates and thresholds change by notification and differ by taxpayer type. Confirm current requirements with ICAI, the official portals and the Acts.

Questions

Questions chartered accountants ask.

Does Verity file returns or generate UDINs?

No. Your team files on the government portals and the signing partner generates the UDIN on ICAI's portal. Verity records the status and the number, and makes sure nothing leaves without it.

Will it replace Tally or my tax software?

No. Your team keeps using them. Verity runs the calendar, documents, assignments, hours and fees around that work.

How does it keep up when rules change?

You or your manager log the change and the clients it touches by registration, entity type or turnover band. Verity creates review tasks for each lead. It does not interpret the law, and your team decides what applies.

I run a five-person practice. Is this too much?

A small practice has the same calendar with fewer people to hold it. We set up only what you need, in the same four weeks, and leave out what you do not.

Can my clients see anything?

Only if you choose. A client can have a view of their own checklist, notices and invoices, and nothing about other clients. We agree this with you during setup.

Can it track audit working papers?

It holds them as records on the assignment stage, with who prepared and who reviewed. It does not replace audit software your firm may use. We agree what sits where during setup.

How does it help with article assistants?

It shows each person's load and keeps a training log, so work and learning are spread fairly and a partner sees who is ready for more.

Who can see my data?

Only the people you allow. Access is set by role and by client, and every view and change is recorded with who made it and when.

How soon will I see something useful?

The compliance calendar and the missing documents list need only your existing client data, so we aim to have them first. Profitability becomes reliable after a few weeks of logged time.

What does it cost?

It depends on the number of staff and clients and what you want set up. Message us and we will give you a clear number after a short conversation about how your practice runs.
