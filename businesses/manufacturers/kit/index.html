Timings are a guide for a cut of about a minute. Each runs in the same order: the problem, the free seven-day demo offer, the change, the owner view, no charge if it does not fit.

    Sales ne date di, production ko baad mein pata chala, aur customer ko late maal mila.

  3. 15 to 25s

    Aap apna business process batayiye. Hum saat din mein, aapke actual business data par, ek customised demo banate hain. Bilkul free.

  4. 25 to 40s

Sales kit

Reaching and talking to manufacturers

Lead with: Late deliveries and the step that caused them

Who to reach

Titles to search
Managing Director · Owner · Director Operations · Plant Head · General Manager Production · Head of Planning · Head of Purchase
Who decides
The owner or managing director decides. The plant head influences, and accounts raises cost questions.
Best business size
A make-to-order factory with 50 to 300 people, several machines or lines, and customers who ask for delivery dates.

First message

Hello, I work with manufacturers on one problem: orders that go late and nobody can say which step caused it. We build a free seven day demo on your own order and job data. How do you decide a delivery date today?

Discovery questions

  1. How do you promise a delivery date to a customer?
  2. Where is the current plan and who updates it?
  3. How do you know a material is short before the line stops?
  4. How do you trace a customer complaint to its batch?
  5. How are scrap and rework recorded?
  6. Who follows overdue customer payments and how?

Objections

We have an ERP already
We do not replace what works. We connect the order, plan and shortage view that the ERP does not show you, and you see it on your data.
Our team will not enter data
We start with the few screens each person needs, in your terms, and reduce what they have to write.
Too busy for a project
The four week plan needs about 8 to 14 owner hours in total. We do the rest with your team.
Cost
Cost depends on scope. We start with the one problem that costs most, and the demo is free.

Demo and proof

Demo to build
A week of jobs on one board, a material shortfall dated to the line, one complaint traced to a lot and an overdue customer list, built from the factory's own order sheet.
Proof of value
Take one late order from last quarter and show which step made it late.

Follow-up message

Thank you for your time. I have built the demo around the late order you described, with the step that caused it. When can your plant head and I go through it for 20 minutes?
