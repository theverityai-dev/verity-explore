5 timed scripts

Short-form scripts

Timings are a guide for a cut of about a minute. Each runs in the same order: the problem, the free seven-day demo offer, the change, the owner view, no charge if it does not fit.

Script 1

Urgent enquiry

  1. 0 to 5s

    Plant ka urgent enquiry aaya. Jawab kisne diya?

  2. 5 to 15s

    Phone, message, email, sab jagah, aur kisi ek ke paas.

  3. 15 to 25s

    Aap apna business process batayiye. Hum saat din mein, aapke actual business data par, ek customised demo banate hain. Bilkul free.

  4. 25 to 40s

    Har enquiry ka record: plant, contact, items aur response time. Limit se upar ruka hua enquiry agle aadmi ko jaata hai.

  5. 40 to 50s

    Breakdown wale plant ko jawab turant milta hai.

  6. 50 to 60s

    Aapki team dekhe, pasand aaye tabhi aage badhiye. Nahi toh koi charge nahi.

Script 2

  1. 0 to 5s

    Quote bheja aur phir kisi ne call nahi kiya.

  2. 5 to 15s

  3. 15 to 25s

    Apne open quote dijiye. Saat din mein free demo.

  4. 25 to 40s

  5. 40 to 50s

    Aapko pata chalta hai kyun haarte hain.

  6. 50 to 60s

    Pasand aaye tabhi aage. Nahi toh koi charge nahi.

Sales kit

Reaching and talking to industrial suppliers

Lead with: Late enquiry response, unfollowed quotes and bills stuck in plant processes

Who to reach

Titles to search
Owner · Managing Director · Director · Head of Sales · General Manager · Accounts Head · Branch Manager
Who decides
The owner decides. The head of sales and accounts head influence.
Best business size
An industrial supply business with 15 to 120 people, many brands and parts, and plant customers on long credit.

First message

Hello, I work with industrial suppliers on enquiries that are answered late and quotes that are never followed up. We build a free seven day demo on your own enquiry and quote data. How do you track the response time to an urgent plant enquiry today?

Discovery questions

  1. How do you make sure every enquiry is answered quickly?
  2. How do you follow up quotes and learn why you lose?
  3. How do you handle brand price changes?
  4. How are rate contracts applied at order time?
  5. How do you keep up with each plant's bill submission steps?
  6. How do you track brand targets and warranty claims?

Objections

We have billing software
We keep it. We add the enquiry, quote, contract and plant-process records that billing software does not hold.
Salesmen will not log enquiries
The record is how they get follow-up reminders and a clear view of their own quotes, which they usually want.
Cost
Cost depends on scope. We start with the problem that costs most, and the demo is free.
Busy season
The four week plan needs about 8 to 14 owner hours in total.

Demo and proof

Demo to build
An enquiry with response time, a quote due for a call, a contract rate applied on a PO with a difference flagged, and a bill submission status, built from the supplier's own data.
Proof of value
Take last month's quotes and show how many were never followed up and why the others were lost.

Follow-up message

Thank you for your time. I have built the demo around the quotes and enquiries you described. When can you and your head of sales go through it for 20 minutes?
