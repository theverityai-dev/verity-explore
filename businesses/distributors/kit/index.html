5 timed scripts

Short-form scripts

Timings are a guide for a cut of about a minute. Each runs in the same order: the problem, the free seven-day demo offer, the change, the owner view, no charge if it does not fit.

Script 1

Beat ka udhaar

  1. 0 to 5s

    Beat par udhaar badhta jaa raha hai, kaun rok raha hai?

  2. 5 to 15s

    Salesman ko order chahiye aur retailer ko credit.

  3. 15 to 25s

    Aap apna business process batayiye. Hum saat din mein, aapke actual business data par, ek customised demo banate hain. Bilkul free.

  4. 25 to 40s

    Har outlet, beat aur salesman ke hisaab se outstanding. Limit paar karne wala order aapke ek tap ka intezaar karta hai, sabse purane bill ke saath.

  5. 40 to 50s

    Faisla order ke waqt hota hai.

  6. 50 to 60s

    Aapki team dekhe, pasand aaye tabhi aage badhiye. Nahi toh koi charge nahi.

Sales kit

Reaching and talking to distributors

Lead with: Retailer credit on the beat, scheme claims and pending company claims

Who to reach

Titles to search
Owner · Managing Director · Director · Head of Sales · Accounts Head · General Manager · Depot Manager
Who decides
The owner decides. The accounts head and sales manager influence.
Best business size
A distribution business with 20 to 150 people, several companies, vans and salesmen on beats.

First message

Hello, I work with distributors on retailer credit, scheme claims and company claims that stay pending. We build a free seven day demo on your own outlet and claim data. How do you track what a salesman has given on credit today?

Discovery questions

  1. How do you check a retailer's credit limit when an order comes in?
  2. How do you settle a salesman's cash against bills at the end of the day?
  3. How do you track scheme claims to the company?
  4. How are damage and expiry claims followed up?
  5. How do you decide which primary orders to accept?
  6. Which company is the most profitable for you after credit and claims?

Objections

We use the company's distributor software
That software shows the company's view. We add your own credit, claim and salesman views.
Salesmen will resist
We start with what makes their month-end statement clear, which they usually want.
Cost
Cost depends on scope. We start with the problem that costs most, and the demo is free.
We are busy
The four week plan needs about 8 to 14 owner hours in total.

Demo and proof

Demo to build
An outlet over its credit limit, a settlement with a cash gap by bill, a scheme claim totalled by outlet and a list of pending claims by age, built from the distributor's own data.
Proof of value
Take one company's last quarter of schemes and claims and show what has not been claimed or credited.

Follow-up message

Thank you for your time. I have built the demo around the scheme and claims you described. When can you and your accounts head go through it for 20 minutes?
