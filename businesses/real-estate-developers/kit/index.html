5 timed scripts

Short-form scripts

Timings are a guide for a cut of about a minute. Each runs in the same order: the problem, the free seven-day demo offer, the change, the owner view, no charge if it does not fit.

Script 1

Visit ka follow up

  1. 0 to 5s

    Site visit hui, follow up kisne kiya?

  2. 5 to 15s

    Executive ke phone mein, owner ko pata nahi.

  3. 15 to 25s

    Aap apna business process batayiye. Hum saat din mein, aapke actual business data par, ek customised demo banate hain. Bilkul free.

  4. 25 to 40s

    Har visit ka record: kaun sa unit pasand aaya, budget, agla kadam. Jis visit ka follow up nahi hua woh list mein.

  5. 40 to 50s

    Sales head ko roz dikhta hai kaun thanda pad raha hai.

  6. 50 to 60s

    Aapki team dekhe, pasand aaye tabhi aage badhiye. Nahi toh koi charge nahi.

Sales kit

Reaching and talking to real estate developers

Lead with: Cold site visits, late demand letters and money stuck in bank tranches

Who to reach

Titles to search
Managing Director · Owner · Director · Head of Sales · Head of Projects · Head of Accounts · Chief Financial Officer
Who decides
The managing director decides. The sales head, project head and finance head influence.
Best business size
A developer with 50 to 500 people, one to ten projects and buyers on stage-linked plans and bank loans.

First message

Hello, I work with real estate developers on site visits that go cold and demands that go out late. We build a free seven day demo on one of your projects. How do you follow up a visit that did not convert?

Discovery questions

  1. How do you follow up site visits and who sees the list?
  2. How do you settle channel partner claims?
  3. How do you hold and release units?
  4. How do you link demands to milestones?
  5. How do you track bank tranches?
  6. How do you prepare quarterly regulatory updates?

Objections

We use a CRM for sales
We keep it and connect the unit, payment and milestone records it does not cover.
Our team is busy at launch
The four week plan needs about 8 to 14 owner hours in total, and we can start with one project.
Cost
Cost depends on scope. We start with one project and the demo is free.
Compliance is with the consultant
We keep the consultant and give them clean records to work from.

Demo and proof

Demo to build
Visits with no follow-up listed, a unit hold expiring, a milestone producing a demand, a loan tranche not yet disbursed and homes with possession at risk.
Proof of value
Take one tower and show overdue demands, stalled bookings and bank tranches pending.

Follow-up message

Thank you for your time. I have built the demo around the project you described, with its visits and demands. When can your head of sales and I go through it for 20 minutes?
