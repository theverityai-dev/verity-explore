5 timed scripts

Short-form scripts

Timings are a guide for a cut of about a minute. Each runs in the same order: the problem, the free seven-day demo offer, the change, the owner view, no charge if it does not fit.

Script 1

Signature ka intezaar

  1. 0 to 5s

    Engineer sign nahi kar raha, bill nahi ban raha.

  2. 5 to 15s

    Kaam ho chuka, measurement kachche note mein, aur paisa atka hua.

  3. 15 to 25s

    Aap apna business process batayiye. Hum saat din mein, aapke actual business data par, ek customised demo banate hain. Bilkul free.

  4. 25 to 40s

    Har kaam ka measurement task: quantity, tarikh, photo, aur client engineer jise sign karna hai. Intezaar wali list alag.

  5. 40 to 50s

    Sign hote hi measurement agle running bill ki line ban jaata hai.

  6. 50 to 60s

    Aapki team dekhe, pasand aaye tabhi aage badhiye. Nahi toh koi charge nahi.

  1. 0 to 5s

  2. 5 to 15s

  3. 15 to 25s

    Apne bills dijiye. Saat din mein free demo.

  4. 25 to 40s

    Har bill ka stage: submitted, checked, certified, passed, paid. Tarikh aur wo insaan jisse baat hui.

  5. 40 to 50s

    Atke hue bill ki list upar.

  6. 50 to 60s

    Pasand aaye tabhi aage. Nahi toh koi charge nahi.

Sales kit

Reaching and talking to contractors

Lead with: Measured work that never becomes a bill, and bills stuck at the client

Who to reach

Titles to search
Owner · Proprietor · Managing Partner · Director · Site Manager · Head of Accounts · Project Manager
Who decides
The owner decides, often alone. A project manager or accounts head influences.
Best business size
A contractor with 15 to 150 people, several sites, sub-contractors and running bills to government or private clients.

First message

Hello, I work with contractors on measured work that never reaches the bill and bills that sit with the client. We build a free seven day demo on your own site data. How do you know today what has been measured but not billed?

Discovery questions

  1. How do you get measurements signed and track the wait?
  2. How do you track bills at the client end?
  3. How do you track retention and release dates?
  4. How do you record variations?
  5. How do you check sub-contractor payments against measured work?
  6. How do you see a site's margin while it runs?

Objections

I do it myself and it works
It works while you are everywhere. We make the same record available to your supervisor and accounts person so it works when you are not.
My supervisors are not computer people
We keep entries short and in the terms they already use.
Too expensive
Cost depends on scope. We start with one site, and the demo is free.
Too busy
The four week plan needs about 8 to 14 owner hours in total.

Demo and proof

Demo to build
A site with measurements waiting for signature, a bill with a measured and billed gap, retention due soon and a sub-contractor payment against measured work.
Proof of value
Take one site and show measured but unbilled work and retention due.

Follow-up message

Thank you for your time. I have built the demo around the site you described, with its measurements and bills. When can you and your site manager go through it for 20 minutes?
